# Content Guidelines

## 1. Brand voice

Lumina should sound:
- Knowledgeable, not boastful.
- Confident, not exaggerated.
- Human, not overly technical.
- Clear, not jargon-heavy.
- Internationally understandable.

## 2. Core message

**Illuminate Your Digital Potential.**

Supporting message:

**Thoughtful Design. Intelligent Engineering.**

Positioning paragraph:

"Lumina Creative is a technology-focused digital studio helping businesses turn ideas and challenges into meaningful digital experiences. We combine web engineering, user-centered design and creative problem-solving to build websites and digital solutions."

Indonesian adaptation:

"Lumina Creative adalah studio teknologi digital yang membantu bisnis mengubah ide dan tantangan menjadi pengalaman digital yang bermakna. Kami menggabungkan rekayasa web, desain berpusat pada pengguna, dan pemecahan masalah kreatif untuk membangun website serta solusi digital."

Review Indonesian copy for natural business language; do not translate word-for-word if it sounds unnatural.

## 3. Service messaging

### Web Development
Outcome: A professional, responsive digital presence that makes it easier for customers to understand a business and get in touch.

### Custom Web Applications
Outcome: Digital systems tailored to business workflows, reducing repetitive manual processes and improving operational visibility.

### UI/UX Design
Outcome: Clearer, more usable digital experiences informed by user needs and evidence.

### Branding & Graphic Design
Outcome: A consistent visual identity that helps a business communicate clearly and professionally.

Avoid guaranteeing sales, rankings or conversion improvements.

## 4. Portfolio integrity

Every project must be classified:
- Client Project.
- Internal Project.
- Research Project.
- Demonstration Project.

Required checks before publishing:
- Permission to show client name/logo.
- Permission to show screenshots.
- Accurate description of Lumina's contribution.
- Accurate technology list.
- Evidence for any numerical result.
- Clear label for prototypes and self-initiated concepts.

If no verified business outcome exists, report deliverables rather than inventing impact.

## 5. Calls to action

Use specific labels:
- Start a Project.
- Discuss Your Website.
- Explore Our Work.
- Request a Consultation.
- Tell Us About Your Project.

Avoid vague labels such as "Click Here" or repeated "Learn More" where a more specific action is possible.

## 6. Bilingual publishing

- English and Indonesian copy must be reviewed.
- Keep the same core meaning, but localize idioms and examples.
- Translate form errors and success messages.
- Localize dates, currency and number formats.
- Do not expose untranslated fallback strings in published pages.
- If a page has no translation, use a deliberate fallback and do not emit incorrect hreflang.

## 7. Claims policy

Do not publish:
- Unverified project counts.
- Unverified satisfaction percentages.
- Unverified average ratings.
- Fabricated testimonials.
- Client logos without permission.
- Unsupported performance or revenue claims.
- AI-generated mockups represented as completed client work.

Prefer concrete proof: screenshots, project scope, deliverables, process, technologies and verified feedback.
